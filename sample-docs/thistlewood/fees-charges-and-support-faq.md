# Thistlewood Bank: Fees, Charges and Customer Support FAQ

## Savings account minimum balance

- Classic Savings Account: monthly average balance of Rs 5,000 in metro and urban branches, and Rs 2,000 in rural branches.
- Zero Balance (Basic Savings Bank Deposit) Account: no minimum balance required.
- Salary Account: no minimum balance while your salary is credited every month.

If the monthly average balance is not maintained, a charge of 5% of the shortfall applies, up to a maximum of Rs 250 per month. Charges never make your balance negative.

## Debit card charges

- Annual fee for the Classic debit card: Rs 199 plus GST, waived in the first year.
- Replacement card: Rs 150 plus GST.
- International usage: 3.5% foreign currency markup.

## ATM transactions

- Thistlewood Bank ATMs: 5 free transactions per month.
- Other banks' ATMs: 3 free transactions per month in metro cities and 5 in other cities.
- After the free limit: Rs 21 plus GST per financial transaction.

## Fund transfer charges

- NEFT, RTGS and IMPS through net banking or the Thistlewood Mobile app: free.
- NEFT at a branch: Rs 2 to Rs 25 plus GST depending on the amount.
- UPI: free.

## Cheque book

The first 25 cheque leaves in a financial year are free. After that, Rs 4 per leaf plus GST.

## Account closure

- Within 14 days of opening: free.
- After 14 days and within 12 months: Rs 500 plus GST.
- After 12 months: free.

## Interest rates on savings accounts

- Balance up to Rs 1 lakh: 2.75% per year.
- Balance above Rs 1 lakh: 3.25% per year.

Interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited every quarter.

## How to contact customer care

- Phone: 1800-000-0000 (toll-free, 8 AM to 10 PM, all days).
- Email: support@thistlewood.example
- Lost or stolen card: block it instantly in the Thistlewood Mobile app, or call 1800-000-0000 at any time (24x7).

## How to raise a complaint

1. Contact customer care or visit your branch. We aim to resolve complaints within 7 working days.
2. If you are not satisfied, write to our Principal Nodal Officer at nodal.officer@thistlewood.example. The Nodal Officer responds within 14 days.
3. If your complaint is not resolved within 30 days, or you are not satisfied with our reply, you can file a complaint with the RBI under the Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme through the RBI's complaint management portal.

## Safety reminder

Thistlewood Bank will never call, email or message you to ask for your OTP, PIN, password, CVV or full card number. Never share these with anyone.
