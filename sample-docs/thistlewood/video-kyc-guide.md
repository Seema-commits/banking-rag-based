# Thistlewood Bank: Video KYC Guide

## What is Video KYC?

Video KYC lets you complete your KYC from home through a live video call with a Thistlewood Bank officer, without visiting a branch. It follows the Video based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) allowed by the Reserve Bank of India. A Video KYC is treated the same as an in-person KYC at a branch.

You can use Video KYC to open a new savings account, to convert a minimum-KYC digital account into a full-KYC account, or to complete your periodic re-KYC.

## Who can use Video KYC?

- Indian residents aged 18 years or above.
- You must be physically present in India during the call. We check your live location (GPS) and IP address.
- NRIs, minors, and joint account holders cannot use Video KYC at present. They must visit a branch.

## What you need before the call

- Your original PAN card (physical card, not a photocopy or image).
- Your Aadhaar number and the mobile number linked to it, for OTP verification. You can use offline Aadhaar XML instead.
- A blank white paper and a blue or black pen, for your signature.
- A smartphone or laptop with a front camera, and a stable internet connection of at least 512 kbps.
- A quiet, well-lit room. Nobody else should be present or helping you during the call.

## Video KYC hours

Video KYC calls are available from 9 AM to 9 PM, Monday to Saturday, except bank holidays. Calls are not available on Sundays.

## Step-by-step process

1. Open the Thistlewood Mobile app or website and tap "Open account with Video KYC".
2. Enter your PAN and verify your Aadhaar with the OTP sent to your linked mobile number.
3. Fill in your personal details, occupation and annual income.
4. Give permission for camera, microphone and location, then tap "Start Video KYC".
5. A Thistlewood officer joins the call. Typical wait time is under 5 minutes.
6. Show your original PAN card to the camera when asked. The officer captures a photo of it.
7. The officer captures your live photo and asks a few simple questions to confirm your details.
8. Sign on the white paper and show it to the camera.
9. The call ends. The full call is recorded and stored securely as required by RBI rules.

The call usually takes 5 to 7 minutes.

## After the call

Our team reviews the recording. If everything is in order, your account is activated within 4 working hours and you receive the account number by SMS and email.

## Why Video KYC can be rejected

Your Video KYC may be rejected if:

- You are outside India during the call, or your location cannot be verified.
- You show a photocopy or a picture of your PAN card instead of the original.
- Your face does not match the photo on your documents or in Aadhaar records.
- Another person is seen or heard helping you during the call.
- The video or audio quality is too poor to verify you.

## What if my call drops?

If your call disconnects, you can restart Video KYC from the app within 72 hours without entering your details again. After 72 hours, you must start a new application. You can attempt Video KYC up to 3 times for one application. After 3 failed attempts, please visit any Thistlewood Bank branch to complete KYC in person.

## Is Video KYC safe?

Yes. The call uses end-to-end encryption and is conducted only by trained Thistlewood Bank officers. Our officers will never ask for your OTP, PIN, password or CVV during the call. If anyone asks for these, end the call and report it to customer care on 1800-000-0000.
