# Thistlewood Bank: Re-KYC and Updating Your Details

## What is re-KYC?

Re-KYC, also called periodic updation of KYC, means confirming or refreshing your KYC details at regular intervals. RBI rules require banks to update KYC periodically based on each customer's risk category.

## How often is re-KYC needed?

Thistlewood Bank follows the RBI schedule for periodic updation:

- High-risk customers: at least once every 2 years.
- Medium-risk customers: at least once every 8 years.
- Low-risk customers: at least once every 10 years.

Your risk category depends on factors such as the type of account, your occupation, and the nature of your transactions. Most salaried and individual savings account customers are in the low-risk category.

We will remind you by SMS, email and app notification when your re-KYC is due. We send at least three reminders before the due date, including at least one by letter to your registered address.

## Re-KYC when nothing has changed

If your KYC details have not changed, you only need to give a self-declaration. You can do this through any of these channels:

- Thistlewood Mobile app: Profile > KYC > "Confirm my details"
- Net banking: Services > Re-KYC self-declaration
- Email from your registered email ID to rekyc@thistlewood.example
- At any Thistlewood Bank branch
- At our ATMs: choose "Other Services" > "Re-KYC"

No documents are needed for a no-change self-declaration.

## Re-KYC when only your address has changed

If only your address has changed, give a self-declaration of the new address through any of the channels above. We will verify the new address within two months, either by sending a letter to it or by a branch visit.

## Re-KYC when other details have changed

If your name, photo, or identity details have changed, or your OVD has expired, you must submit a fresh OVD and complete KYC again. You can do this at a branch or through Video KYC.

## What happens if I miss my re-KYC date?

If re-KYC is not completed by the due date, we may apply a partial freeze on your account after giving you notice:

- First, we stop all credits (deposits) into the account, except interest, government benefits and refunds.
- If re-KYC is still not completed within 3 months after that, we also stop debits (withdrawals).

Your money stays safe in your account. All restrictions are removed within 2 working days after you complete re-KYC.

## Updating your mobile number

To change your registered mobile number, visit a branch with an OVD, or use net banking with your debit card details and an OTP sent to your old number. For your safety, high-value online transfers are blocked for 24 hours after a mobile number change.

## Updating your name

To change your name, for example after marriage, submit a request at a branch with proof of the name change, such as a marriage certificate or gazette notification, along with an updated OVD in the new name.

## Updating your email ID

You can update your email ID in the Thistlewood Mobile app under Profile > Contact details. An OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number to confirm.
